We are pleased to announce that the Centre for Research in Astrophysique of Québec has been awarded funding under the FRQ’s 2026–2027 Strategic Clusters competition – NT sector.

With this new funding comes a new brand identity. We will now be known as AstroQuébec, and today we are unveiling our new website and logo. Here you will find news about the cluster, our activities and a directory of our members.
