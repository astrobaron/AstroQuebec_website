## Titre

First Light with the CHORD Pathfinder Array

## Résumé

The Canadian Hydrogen Observatory and Radio-transient Detector, or CHORD, is a new radio telescope facility under construction at the Dominion Radio Astrophysical Observatory near Penticton, British Columbia. Once completed, CHORD will provide world-leading capabilities in radio transients and 21 cm surveys, among other key science cases. Like many new radio telescopes, CHORD is an interferometer that spreads its collecting area across a large number of smaller elements—specifically, 6-meter diameter dishes—rather than relying on a single large aperture. Operationally, this modular design allows the instrument to be commissioned incrementally as new dishes are added. Recently, CHORD achieved the milestone of bringing online its first 64 dishes out of an eventual total of 512 dishes at the core site. While commissioning activities with this subset of dishes (dubbed the CHORD Pathfinder) are still underway, preliminary data are very encouraging. Following an overview of the instrument, its construction, and current status, this talk will offer a peek at some of the first observations.
