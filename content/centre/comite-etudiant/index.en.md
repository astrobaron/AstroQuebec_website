The Student and Postdoctoral Committee (CEP) of the Centre for Research in Astrophysics of Quebec is responsible for developing, coordinating, and carrying out activities aimed at improving the training and integration of student and postdoctoral members of the Centre.

The CEP is made up of student members (MSc and PhD) and postdoctoral fellows from our four universities (Montréal, Laval, McGill, and Bishop’s).

For the 2026-2027 academic year, the members of the CEP are:

- Avidaan Srivastava, President, Université de Montréal
- Laurie Amen, McGill University
- Mélissa St-Pierre, Université Laval
- Sam Wong, McGill University
- Luna Zagorac, McGill University
