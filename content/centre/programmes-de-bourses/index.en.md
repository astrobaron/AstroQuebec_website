AstroQuébec offers funding programs that aim to support the training of the next generation of researchers, encourage scientific collaboration across institutions and disciplines, and raise the profile of astrophysics research in Quebec and internationally.

For the group’s co-researchers, AstroQuébec funds new collaborative and interdisciplinary research initiatives. These projects must involve the co-supervision of students by members from different institutions or research axes. The group also provides support for early-career professors to establish international collaborations.

For students, the group supports research stays at its member institutions and offers scholarships to enable them to present their work at international conferences, encouraging mobility and the development of new collaborations. AstroQuébec also provides funding for postdoctoral fellows to give a seminar at each of its member institutions.

These programs help create a dynamic and collaborative research environment and support the development of the next generation of scientists.
