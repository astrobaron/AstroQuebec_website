The Centre for Research in Astrophysics of Quebec (AstroQuébec) is an interdisciplinary grouping that addresses the major questions of modern astrophysics and is funded by the Fonds de recherche du Québec. It brings together all of Quebec’s expertise in astrophysics and strengthens it through collaboration with specialists in planetary sciences, atmospheric sciences, machine learning and engineering, particle physics, and subatomic physics.

It brings together co-researchers from partner universities (Université de Montréal, McGill University, Université Laval, Université Bishop’s) and collaborating institutions (Cégep de Bois-de-Boulogne, Planétarium Rio Tinto Alcan), as well as collaborating members from academic, industrial, and governmental sectors, along with students at the master’s and doctoral levels and postdoctoral fellows. All of them work around the theme “Understanding the Universe” and explore the main areas of modern astrophysics in an effort to answer fundamental questions related to the formation and evolution of our Universe, its contents, and our place within it.

Our research is organized around two axes. Axis 1 – Understanding the Universe – aims to advance knowledge about planetary systems and the origin of life, the life cycle of stars, the formation and evolution of galaxies, and the origin and large-scale structure of the Universe. Axis 2 – Pushing the Frontiers – allows researchers to explore the Universe beyond current limits through the construction and use of larger and more powerful telescopes, the development of new instruments and data analysis tools at the cutting edge of technology, the application of machine learning to massive datasets, the computation of more complete and detailed numerical models of the observable Universe, and the development of new theories.

AstroQuébec’s governance is based on three bodies: the *Conseil de gouvernance*, the *Conseil scientifique (CS)* and the *comité étudiant et postdoctoral (CÉP)*.

The CS brings together the Director, one co-researcher from each university, a member of the CEP, and the Coordinator. It develops the scientific programme, oversees activities, and supports the Centre’s development. The CÉP of the Centre for Research in Astrophysics of Quebec is mandated to develop, coordinate, and carry out activities that enhance the training and integration of the Centre’s student and postdoctoral members.

The CÉP is made up of students (MSc and PhD) and postdoctoral fellows from our four universities (Université de Montréal, Université Laval, McGill University, and Bishop’s University).
