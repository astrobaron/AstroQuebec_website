## Research project

Nicolas Cowan’s research aims to better understand and solve the complex problems facing the science of exoplanets by taking advantage of their diversity. He uses high-precision measurements, obtained in the visible and infrared light spectra, to study exoplanet atmospheres and map these distant worlds. In addition to his empirical research into the climate of hot Jupiters, which he conducts using the James Webb Space Telescope and ground-based telescopes in Hawaii and Chile, he contributes to the planning of future scientific missions. In particular, Nicolas Cowan leads the Canadian science team for the European Space Agency’s Ariel mission. Nicolas Cowan is one of the organisers of the ExoClimes conferences.
