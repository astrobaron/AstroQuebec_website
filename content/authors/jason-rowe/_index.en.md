## Research project

Jason Rowe's research focuses primarily on the detection and characterisation of exoplanets. Previously, he worked at the University of Montreal as a researcher on the JWST/NIRISS team. He is also affiliated with the SETI Institute, participates in NASA’s Kepler mission and is a member of the scientific team for the MOST mission. He is also a member of the CASCA Joint Committee on Space Astronomy and is one of CASCA’s Westar lecturers. In July 2016, he received NASA’s Medal for Exceptional Scientific Excellence for his research on exoplanets as a member of the Kepler mission’s science team.
