## Research project

Kenneth Ragan’s research focuses on high-energy physics and gamma-ray astronomy. He is a member of the VERITAS collaboration, which operates an array of four multi-imaging telescopes, each 12 metres in diameter. This is the world’s most sensitive Cherenkov light detector. High-energy gamma rays strike the upper atmosphere and create showers of charged secondary particles. These secondary particles are energetic enough to travel faster than the (local) speed of light in the upper atmosphere and emit Cherenkov radiation. By measuring the properties of this light (intensity and direction), VERITAS is able to deduce the direction and energy of the source responsible for the gamma-ray emission.
