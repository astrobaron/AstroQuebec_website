## Research project

Simon Thibault leads the Laboratory for Optical Engineering Research (LRIO) at Université Laval — the only Canadian research laboratory offering graduate-level (master's and doctoral) training in optical design. Within this laboratory, he leads several research projects on theoretical and experimental concepts for developing solutions across various fields of modern optics, including instrumentation for astronomy and space exploration, medical applications, security and surveillance, remote sensing, digital vision, industrial inspection, military systems, and lighting. As holder of the Industrial Research Chair in Optical Design, his research activities address modern questions in optical design and engineering. He and his team focus on implementing original optical design techniques, novel imaging concepts, innovative test setups, new approaches to assembly, advanced freeform surface manufacturing methods, and the design of innovative architectures for optomechanics.
