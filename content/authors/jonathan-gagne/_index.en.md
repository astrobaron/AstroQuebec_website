## Research project

Jonathan Gagné works solar neighborhood stars, brown dwarfs and exoplanets with a specific focus on ages and coeval stellar moving groups. He works with large data sets such as Gaia and SPHEREx combined with ground- and space-based telescope data to uncover the fundamental properties of isolated objects with masses similar to gas giant exoplanets that do not orbit a star, often called “planemos”.
