## Research project

Hugo Martel’s research focuses on the origin and evolution of galaxies and the interaction of galaxies with their surrounding environment. This is primarily theoretical work based on high-performance numerical simulations. This research programme comprises the following elements: 1) the history of star formation and metal enrichment within galaxies, resulting from their long-term evolution; particular attention is paid to the consequences of the presence of a galactic bar or an active galactic nucleus; 2) the influence of the environment on the evolution of galaxies, including phenomena such as accretion from the intergalactic medium, minor mergers with other galaxies, perturbations caused by tidal forces, and the loss of gas due to ram pressure in dense environments; 3) the deposition of energy and metal-rich gas into the intergalactic medium by galactic winds, the spatial distribution of these metals, and the feedback effect of galactic winds, and their influence on the subsequent formation of galaxies.
