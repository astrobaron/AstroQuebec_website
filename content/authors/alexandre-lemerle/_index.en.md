## Research project

Alexandre Lemerle is developing a hybrid model of the solar activity cycle, coupling an existing 2D simulation of the activity cycle with a second simulation of the evolution of the surface magnetic field, including the emergence and dissipation of sunspots. The (relative) simplicity of such a coupled simulation, compared with a full three-dimensional turbulent MHD simulation of the Sun’s convective zone, will enable the production of long time series that can be used as input for simulations of variations in solar irradiance spanning several centuries. In particular, it will be of interest to trace the data back to the Maunder Minimum, a period of solar inactivity spanning from around 1645 to 1715, which coincides with the so-called ‘Little Ice Age’, well known in climatology. The project therefore has potential implications that go far beyond the modelling of the solar activity cycle and thus fits very well within the current framework of environmental studies on climate change.
